short_bio: "Komonazmuk is Bristol-born DJ and producer Keiren Lomax. Turned on to rave by Easy Groove at Fantazia, he came up through Bristol's jungle scene as a resident at the Apex party and released on Moving Shadow, Tech Itch and his own Ice Minus Recordings. As Komonazmuk he made dark dubstep and post-dubstep for HENCH, Tempa and ApplePips, remixed Bonobo and Emika, and performed on Mary Anne Hobbs's Bristol Rise Up show on Radio 1. He now records as Komon, moving toward techno."
