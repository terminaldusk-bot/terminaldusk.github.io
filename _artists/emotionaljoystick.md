short_bio: "Emotional Joystick is the experimental electronic dance project of Thomas Wincek, who studied sound and improvisation with Nicolas Collins, John Corbett and Rob Drinkwater. The project brings improvisation and sonic exploration into contemporary dance music, and has taken him on tours of the US and Europe alongside acts such as Tortoise. Wincek is also a member of Volcano Choir with Justin Vernon and the force behind the Wisconsin band All Tiny Creatures."
