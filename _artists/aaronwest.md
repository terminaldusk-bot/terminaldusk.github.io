short_bio: International composer and auditory artist Aaron West constructs sonic environments that explore the "long echo" of our sonic past. Spanning performances and collaborations across the U.S., Europe, and Russia, his practice utilizes analog tools—including cassettes and reel-to-reel loops—to blend acoustic piano and violin with analog processing. By prioritizing texture, decay, and organic evolution, West treats sound as a living, decaying entity, crafting works that range from abrasive ruptures to expansive meditations where instrumentation dissolves into static.
