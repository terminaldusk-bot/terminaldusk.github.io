short_bio: "Babylon System is the dubstep duo of San Francisco's Roommate and his long-time friend No Thing, a Los Angeles artist. Formed in 2006, as dubstep was first reaching the US, they were behind some of the earliest American dubstep releases, including \"50 Blocks Away\", \"Examination of Time\" and \"Loaded\". Their aggressive, reggae-infused sound drew support from N-Type, Plastician, Skream and Bassnectar."
