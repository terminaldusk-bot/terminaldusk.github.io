short_bio: "Cardopusher is Luis Garbán, a DJ and producer born in Caracas, Venezuela, and based in Barcelona since 2008. His early productions leaned on electro and acid house, and his sound has since taken in breakcore, dubstep, EBM, garage and house, with Latin American influences throughout. A Red Bull Music Academy alumnus, he co-founded the Classicworks label, released the albums Manipulator and New Cult Fear on Boysnoize Records, put out the Immaculate Poison EP on John Frusciante's Evar Records in 2023, and also records as Safety Trance."
