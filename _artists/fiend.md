short_bio: "Fiend is Steve Vasquez, a DJ and producer from Rialto in California's Inland Empire. He started spinning hardcore techno and acid in the early 1990s, was playing Los Angeles underground events by 1995, and went on to make records for labels around the world. He is part of Darkmatter Soundsystem, the LA collective devoted to extreme electronics and splintered beats, which released his Tools for Tomorrow EP; has helped run the Devil Times Nine label; and co-founded the speedcore label Kill or Be Slaughtered with Arcid. He has also recorded as Abnormalities."
