short_bio: "Not Breathing is the project of Dave Wright, an electronics engineer who builds his own instruments. Beginning in the late 1980s with a series of self-distributed cassettes, it has grown to six full-length CDs, several vinyl releases, more than forty compilation appearances and over 300 live performances across six US tours. Wright spent six years with Chicago's Invisible Records and collaborated with Dead Voices On Air. He is based in Tucson, Arizona."
