short_bio: "Droid Sector is a veteran Melbourne producer known for deep, dark and atmospheric dubstep. After his first 12\" on Tech Itch's Ascension imprint in 2008, he became one of Australia's most internationally recognised dubstep artists, releasing on Paradise Lost, Subway, Shift and Gradient Audio, headlining across Australia and New Zealand, and supporting acts such as Kryptic Minds, SPL and Noah D. He co-owns the label Requiem Audio with Tim Boot. The project has been on hiatus since 2014 while he pursues other musical interests."
