short_bio: "Math Head was the alias of Ben Deitz (died 2015), a New York producer and founding member of the Trouble & Bass club collective. He began making breakcore as Math Head, taking the name from a character in Paul Pope's comic The One Trick Rip-Off, and went on to merge breakcore's breaks and kicks with heavy New York bass. He later also recorded as Passions, and under both names released on labels including Trouble & Bass and Kitsuné."
