short_bio: "The Gasman is Portsmouth producer Christopher Reeves, maker of eerie, playful electronica shaped by teenage breakbeat rave, early Aphex Twin, the Rephlex label, jungle and classical music. Through the 2000s he was a mainstay of Planet Mu, releasing seven albums there, from his debut Remedial (2003), drawn from three demo CDs, through The Grand Electric Palace of Variety, This One's for You and Superlife to Powerpoints (2010). Since then he has turned to intricate piano pieces on Hiding Place (2012), '80s synth-pop and house on Aeriform (2016), and all of it at once on Controlled Hallucination (2018)."
