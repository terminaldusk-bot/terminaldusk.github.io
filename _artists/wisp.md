short_bio: "Wisp is the best-known alias of Reid W. Dunn, a producer from Niagara Falls, New York. His music ranges from jungle and experimental drum & bass to ambient and downtempo, with a strong streak of retro video-game nostalgia and a knack for precision-drilled snares under drifting melodies. He started in 2003 by giving away MP3s through netlabels, worked with Sublight Records, and signed to Rephlex in 2008; albums include The Shimmering Hour, Honor Beats and NRTHNDR. Dunn also records as Lich, skønflap, Havaer and Dwaallicht."
