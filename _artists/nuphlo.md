short_bio: "Nuphlo is Leeds producer and DJ Mannie Sandhu. He started in 2001 with a mix show on Leeds' Fever FM playing Asian Underground and drum & bass, and turned to production so he could play exclusive tunes. Inspired by Black Star Liner, TJ Rehmi and Badmarsh & Shri, he balances eastern melodies, inventive beats and low subs. He has released on Studio Rockers, including the 40 Thieves EP with Tony \"Moody Boyz\" Thorpe, remixed State of Bengal and Foreign Beggars, and been praised by BBC Radio 1's Bobby Friction."
