short_bio: "Line 47 is the long-running project of Scott Weber, co-founder of Terminal Dusk. He started as a percussionist in a noise/post-rock band before moving to electronics and found sound, building early pieces from old turntables, tape loops and homemade lock grooves. Moving to the computer, the music grew more intricate, combining breakcore, drum & bass and experimental textures with jungle influences and Nintendo-esque melodies. He also records as Fault."
