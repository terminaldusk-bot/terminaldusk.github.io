short_bio: "Ochre is the project of Christopher Leary, who has been making melancholic, melodic electronica since 2001. Originally based in Newcastle, England, and now in The Hague, he has released albums on Toytronic, Benbecula and Shipwrec, most recently Oversail. The name began as the title of his academic work at Newcastle College, a variation on Oaker, the Manchester street where he spent his early childhood. Leary also holds a master's degree in music."
